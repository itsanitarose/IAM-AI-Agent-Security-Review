# IAM Job Requirements Map

Project: Northwind Health | AI Agent Security Review

## Overview

This project was developed by reverse-engineering common requirements found in entry-level and junior Identity and Access Management (IAM) job descriptions.

The focus is on non-human identities, least-privilege access, credential security, identity ownership, and access risk assessment.

## Value Demonstration

The Northwind Health AI agent assessment demonstrates the ability to review a non-human identity’s permissions, assess credential and ownership risks, and identify suspicious access activity.

The assessment connects findings to practical recommendations for restricted access, credential management, monitoring, and human approval of sensitive actions.
