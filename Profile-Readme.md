# Anita Rose | Identity & Access Management | AI Security

*Because in the age of AI, defence must be as smart as the threat.*

I'm moving from customer operations into cybersecurity, building my career around Identity and Access Management (IAM).

My work focuses on access reviews, role-based access control, least privilege, identity governance and non-human identity security.

I'm particularly interested in securing AI agents and the identities, credentials and permissions that enable them to operate within enterprise environments.
