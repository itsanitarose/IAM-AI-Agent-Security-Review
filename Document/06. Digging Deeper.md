# 06. Digging Deeper

## Should an AI Agent Ever Approve Refunds on Its Own?

### 1. My Starting Perspective

My initial answer was no.

That came from my experience in BT's billing and payment support teams. Having permission to issue a refund did not automatically mean a customer was entitled to one. I had to investigate the account and decide whether the refund was justified.

Sometimes the visible account information did not tell the full story. Reviewing the customer's history, contract or sales call could change the outcome.

That experience made me cautious about giving an AI agent independent refund authority. But I wanted to examine the security boundaries rather than stop at my first reaction.

### 2. Permission Is Not the Same as Accountability

Feedback from a CISO on my write up and thought process raised another question:

**If an organisation allows an AI agent to approve refunds, who remains responsible when the decision is wrong?**

An organisation cannot simply wash its hands of responsibility by saying the AI made the decision.

I wouldn't consider a transaction limit alone sufficient. Before recommending automation, I would want to see:

| Area | What I Would Expect |
|:-----|:--------------------|
| **Accountability** | A named business owner responsible for the refund policy, permitted automation and corrective action. |
| **Human oversight** | Authorised staff who can review exceptions, challenge decisions and suspend automated processing when necessary. |
| **Decision records** | Evidence of the payment, eligibility checks, policy applied, refund amount, recipient and final transaction result. |
| **Sampling** | Regular human review of a defined sample of automated refunds, alongside targeted checks of unusual transactions and complaints. |
| **Feedback and correction** | A process for investigating mistakes, correcting outcomes and changing or suspending the automation when problems are identified. |

These are the oversight measures I would recommend, not controls demonstrated in the SupportBot scenario.
